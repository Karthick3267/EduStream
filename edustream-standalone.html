Documents REV-09

Income Certificate Standards

Must be issued by Tehsildar, SDM, or official State e-District portal. Affidavits on stamp paper and private salary slips are strictly rejected as Defective. Must be issued after April 1, 2025.

Source: Ministry of Social Justice Circular #2026/09
Disbursement PFMS-DBT

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) & UTR

Payments are cleared via Reserve Bank of India / PFMS. Bank accounts must be NPCI Aadhaar-seeded. Once settled, an immutable 16-character UTR number is generated and linked to the student receipt.

Source: PFMS & NPCI Master Directive 2026
Eligibility MCM-2026

Merit-cum-Means Grant Criteria

Minimum 75% marks in previous qualifying exam. Family annual income ceiling: ₹3,00,000. Sanction grant: ₹50,000 disbursed directly to student bank account.

Source: Central Directorate of Higher Education
